Privacy Policy — Recycling Tycoon

Effective date: November 12, 2026

MEA Dev Studio operates Recycling Tycoon (Android package: com.recyclingtycoon.game). For privacy questions, contact meadevstudio@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current offline Android release. Online accounts, cloud saves, advertising, in-app payments and remote analytics are not enabled in this release.

Data processed on your device

The game stores progress, game settings and save-recovery information locally on your device so you can continue playing. These records are not automatically transmitted to MEA Dev Studio or shared with third parties by the app.

An optional local performance measurement setting is off by default. When enabled, it keeps a limited number of timing samples and gameplay/error events in device memory. These measurements are not sent over the network. Turning the setting off clears the measurements.

Data collection, sharing and permissions

The current offline app does not collect or share user data off your device. It does not include active advertising, tracking, remote analytics or crash-reporting services. It does not request internet, location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions, and it does not require an account.

Backup and report files

You may choose to export game backups or a local measurement report. Backups contain game progress and settings; measurement reports contain the local measurements described above. Android's system file picker lets you choose where to save or open a file, without giving the game broad access to your files.

Exports are not automatically uploaded to MEA Dev Studio. If you choose a cloud storage provider or share a file yourself, that transfer is controlled by you and the selected service. Its own terms and privacy policy apply. Share backups and reports only with people and services you trust.

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain on your device until you reset them or remove the app's stored data. Starting a new game resets game progress. You can remove the app's local data through Android's app storage settings or by uninstalling the app. Exported backups and reports must be deleted separately from the locations where you saved them. The app does not provide an automatic cloud backup or an online account to delete.

Support communications

If you voluntarily email MEA Dev Studio, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you send. We use that information to respond to your support or privacy request and retain it only as long as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. Do not send passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting meadevstudio@gmail.com.

Children

The offline app does not request personal information from children. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us through a support message, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Other services and policy changes

Google Play, your email provider and any storage service you choose operate under their own privacy policies. This policy describes MEA Dev Studio's handling of information in connection with the app.

If a future release changes the app's data practices, we will update this policy and the applicable store disclosures. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
